Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

The short version. Fjellberg: Step Counter counts your steps, tracks your walks, and stores your plan on your device. There are no accounts and no sign-up. Nothing you enter leaves your phone except what you allow through ads (free users) or the map tiles service. We never see your data.

1. Who we are

Fjellberg: Step Counter (the “App”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is developed and published by Daifalla Harkat. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at daifalla.harkat2003@gmail.com.

2. Age requirement

The App is for people 18 years and older. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. The onboarding checks your age before the App can be used.

3. Data stored on your device — and only on your device

Every piece of data the App keeps lives in the App’s private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to any server we own.

  • Profile details — gender, age, height, current weight, goal weight, goal type, daily step target and pace you chose during onboarding.
  • Step history — your daily step counts and goal completions in an on-device database.
  • Saved walk summaries — date, duration, distance walked and steps taken for each finished walk.
  • GPS route points — these exist only in memory while you are walking. They are never saved as coordinates.
  • One weight value — the current weight you set for calculations. There is no permanent weigh-in log.

Delete everything: clear the App’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Fjellberg → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App. That permanently deletes your profile, steps history and saved walks (except any copies in your own Google backup, see below).

4. Android Auto Backup

The App allows Android Auto Backup. That means Android may back up the App’s on-device data (for example your profile and step history) to your own Google account, under your device’s backup settings. This is handled by Google, not by us and not by the App. You can turn it off in your device’s backup settings at any time.

6. Map tiles — Esri

The walk map is provided by Esri World Dark Gray Canvas tiles, loaded from Esri’s server. When the map is opened, your device connects to Esri and Esri can see your device’s IP address (and approximate location derived from it), the way any website’s server can. We don’t control that data and we don’t receive it. Read Esri’s privacy policy: esri.com/privacy.

7. Advertising (free users)

If you use the App for free, a small, non-intrusive Google AdMob banner appears on the Home screen (next to the weekday/date). No banner is ever shown during a walk, on the plan screens, or on the challenge cards. Premium subscribers see no ads at all.

Depending on your consent and region, Google may collect:

  • your advertising ID (a resettable ID),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • app interactions (how you interact with the App and with ads),
  • crash logs and diagnostics,

to serve ads, measure ad performance, personalize ads (if you allow it) and protect against fraud — and only for that. Google’s partner sites policy explains their practices: How Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites or apps.

Reset or opt out of personalised ads:

  • Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID (or delete/reset the advertising ID on newer Android versions), or
  • turn off Opt out of personalised ads from the same screen.

8. Your consent (UMP)

In the EEA, UK and other regions where consent is required, the App shows Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) on first launch, before any ads are loaded. The choices you make control whether ads are shown and whether they’re personalised.

  • To see the consent form again and change your decision, clear the App’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Fjellberg → Storage → Clear data, then reopen the App. The consent form will appear again.
  • You can also opt out of personalised ads at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads (and in your Google account at adssettings.google.com).

9. Premium subscriptions

The premium plan is a paid subscription processed by Google Play Billing:

  • Monthly ($4.99/month) or yearly ($19.99/year), with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan (new subscribers, subject to Google’s offer rules).
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your card number, bank details or billing address.
  • On your phone the App stores only a simple premium on/off flag. The amount you paid, your payment method and your purchase history stay with Google.
  • Cancel anytime: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Fjellberg. You keep premium until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Google under its refund policy.
  • Premium members see no ads.

10. Health information disclaimer

Fjellberg is a general-purpose fitness tracker for walking. It is not a medical device and it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Speak to a healthcare professional before starting or changing any exercise programme, especially if you have a medical condition. Step counts and calorie estimates are approximations.

11. What we never do

  • No accounts. No sign-in. No email or phone number collected from you.
  • No analytics SDK added by us. We don’t profile you.
  • No selling, renting or swapping your data.
  • No sharing of what’s on your device — it stays on your device unless you opt into backup (section 4) or you allow ads (section 7).
